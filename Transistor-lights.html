Transistor Lights

A deep dive into the two-transistor astable multivibrator — from schematic capture to routed board.

Circuit Analysis

How it works: The two BC547 transistors form a cross-coupled flip-flop. When Q1 conducts, its collector voltage drops, pulling Q2's base low through C2 — cutting Q2 off. C1 then charges through R3 until Q2's base voltage rises enough to turn it on, flipping the state.

Timing: The oscillation frequency is determined by R3/R4 and C1/C2. With 47kΩ and 10µF, the LEDs alternate at roughly 0.8Hz — visible to the eye.
